# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in the "Price To Value" mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All calculations performed within the app are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to any servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services
Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Specifically, this app uses:

* **Google AdMob / AdSense:** These services use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on your interests. You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting [Google’s Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 3. Log Data
In the event of an app crash, we may receive basic technical logs (via Google Play Console) to help us fix the issue. This data does not contain personal identification and is used solely for debugging.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
